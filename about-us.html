Our Story

Born from the frustration of seeing talented traders struggle with inconsistent results, TradePilot emerged as the solution to bridge the gap between institutional trading capabilities and retail accessibility.

Our founders, having worked at top-tier financial institutions like Goldman Sachs, Citadel, and Two Sigma, witnessed firsthand the power of algorithmic trading and advanced market analysis tools used by hedge funds and investment banks.

We believed that every trader deserved access to the same caliber of market intelligence, risk management, and signal generation that drives institutional success. TradePilot is the manifestation of that belief.

Our Journey

Key milestones in TradePilot's evolution from startup to industry leader

Q1 2022

Company Founded

TradePilot was founded by former Wall Street veterans with a vision to democratize institutional-grade trading signals.

Beta Platform Launch

Launched our beta platform with 100 select traders, achieving 85% win rate in the first 3 months of operation.
