Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about TradePilot

How do I start using TradePilot signals?
Sign up for a free trial account, complete the onboarding process, and you'll start receiving signals immediately. We recommend starting with our paper trading feature to familiarize yourself with the platform.
What is the accuracy rate of your signals?
Our signals maintain an average accuracy rate of 89.2% across all market conditions. This performance is tracked transparently and audited by third-party firms for verification.
Can I use the API to automate trading?
Yes, our Professional and Enterprise plans include API access that allows you to integrate our signals with your trading systems for automated execution. Full documentation is available in our developer portal.
What markets and assets do you cover?
We currently cover 150+ cryptocurrency pairs, major forex pairs, and select equity markets. Our focus is on high-liquidity assets to ensure reliable execution of our signals.
Do you offer customer support?
Yes, we offer 24/7 support via live chat, email, and phone. Professional subscribers get priority support, while Enterprise customers have dedicated account managers.
