Compliance & Security

TradePilot maintains the highest standards of compliance, security, and regulatory adherence to protect our users and meet enterprise requirements.

SOC 2 Certified ISO 27001 Compliant GDPR Compliant

Security Certifications

SOC 2 Type II

Audited controls for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy of customer data.

Certified: December 2024

ISO 27001

International standard for information security management systems, ensuring systematic approach to managing sensitive information.

Certified: November 2024

GDPR Compliance

Full compliance with General Data Protection Regulation for European users' data privacy and protection rights.

Verified: Ongoing

Security Framework

TradePilot implements a comprehensive security framework designed to protect user data, maintain system integrity, and ensure regulatory compliance across all operations.

Key Security Measures:

  • 256-bit AES encryption for data at rest and in transit
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all user accounts
  • Regular third-party security audits and penetration testing
  • 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
  • Zero-trust network architecture
  • Regular security awareness training for all staff

Data Protection

We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of all user data in accordance with applicable data protection laws and industry best practices.

Data Collection & Usage:

  • Minimal data collection principle - only necessary information
  • Explicit consent for all data processing activities
  • Right to access, rectify, and delete personal data
  • Data portability in standard formats
  • Regular data retention policy reviews

Data Storage & Processing:

  • Data centers located in jurisdictions with strong privacy laws
  • Encrypted databases with restricted access controls
  • Regular data backups and disaster recovery procedures
  • Data anonymization and pseudonymization techniques

Regulatory Compliance

TradePilot operates in compliance with relevant financial services regulations and maintains appropriate licenses where required.

Jurisdictional Compliance:

FINRA compliance, AML/KYC procedures

Important Notice:

TradePilot services may not be available in all jurisdictions. We continuously monitor regulatory developments and adjust our services to maintain compliance. Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with local laws.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

TradePilot maintains a comprehensive AML program to prevent money laundering and terrorist financing in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

AML Procedures:

  • Customer identification and verification (KYC)
  • Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers
  • Ongoing transaction monitoring and reporting
  • Suspicious activity reporting to authorities
  • Regular AML training for all staff
  • Independent AML compliance officer

Audit & Transparency

We believe in transparency and accountability. TradePilot undergoes regular independent audits and maintains detailed compliance documentation.

Audit Schedule:

  • Annual: SOC 2 Type II audits
  • Annual: ISO 27001 certification review
  • Quarterly: Internal security assessments
  • Bi-annual: Third-party penetration testing
  • Ongoing: Compliance monitoring and reporting

Transparency Reports:

Annual transparency reports are available to institutional clients and regulatory authorities upon request. These reports include security metrics, compliance status, and incident summaries.
