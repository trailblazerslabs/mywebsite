Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are our top priorities. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in compliance with GDPR and international privacy laws.

GDPR Compliant • Last Updated: December 2024

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with the best trading signals experience while respecting your privacy.

Personal Information:

  • Email address and account credentials
  • Name and contact information
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communication preferences

Usage Information:

  • Platform usage patterns and analytics
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and location data
  • Performance metrics and feedback

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for legitimate business purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Provide trading signals and platform access
  • Account Management: Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Customer Support: Respond to inquiries and provide assistance
  • Platform Improvement: Analyze usage to enhance user experience
  • Security: Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Legal Compliance: Meet regulatory requirements

We Never:

  • Share your trading data with competitors

Data Security
