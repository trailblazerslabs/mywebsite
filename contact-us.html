Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting TradePilot

How quickly will I get a response?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2 hours during business hours. For urgent technical support, our 24/7 live chat provides immediate assistance. Enterprise customers get priority support with guaranteed response times.
Can I schedule a demo or consultation?
Absolutely! Contact our sales team to schedule a personalized demo of TradePilot. We offer one-on-one consultations to show you how our signals can fit your trading strategy and answer any specific questions you have.
Do you offer phone support?
Phone support is available for Professional and Enterprise subscribers. All users can access our 24/7 live chat support, which is often faster than phone calls. For urgent issues, live chat connects you directly with our technical team.
How can I provide feedback or suggestions?
We love hearing from our users! Send feedback through the contact form above, email us at feedback@tradepilot.io, or join our Discord community where you can share ideas directly with our development team and other traders.
Where can I find technical documentation?
Our comprehensive documentation is available at docs.tradepilot.io. For API documentation, visit api.tradepilot.io. If you can't find what you need, contact our developer support team at developers@tradepilot.io.
