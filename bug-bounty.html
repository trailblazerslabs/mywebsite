Guidelines & Rules

Please follow these guidelines to ensure responsible disclosure

Responsible Disclosure

Report vulnerabilities privately and give us reasonable time to fix them before public disclosure.

No Data Access

Do not access, modify, or delete user data. Test only on your own accounts when possible.

Detailed Reports

Provide clear steps to reproduce, impact assessment, and suggested fixes.

Response Time

We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and provide regular updates on progress.

Hall of Fame

Researchers who responsibly disclose vulnerabilities are acknowledged on our security page.

Legal Protection

Good faith security research is protected under our responsible disclosure policy.
